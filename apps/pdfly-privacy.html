Privacy Policy for PDFly

Last Updated: August 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Processing (Zero Document Uploads)

PDFly Enterprise Document Utility is designed with privacy as the core foundation. All document processing (merging, compressing, organizing, splitting, watermarking, OCR text extraction, and encryption) occurs entirely locally within your device's memory and browser sandbox.

We do not operate servers that receive, process, read, transmit, or store any of your files or document contents.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To keep the application free, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process non-personally identifiable technical data to deliver and optimize relevant ads:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (such as Google Advertising ID or IDFA), device model, OS version.
  • Diagnostic & Region Data: IP address (coarse location to verify jurisdiction and provide localized consent).

3. European Privacy Regulations (GDPR & IAB TCF v2.2)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom:

  • We implement the certified Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK adhering to IAB Europe TCF v2.2 standards.
  • Users are presented with a consent dialog upon launch to accept, reject, or customize ad preferences.
  • Right to Revoke: You can modify or withdraw consent at any time via Settings ➔ Privacy & Ad Preferences (GDPR Consent) in the app.

4. California & US State Privacy Laws (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell personal information or document data. US users can opt-out of behavioral ad tracking via the app's consent settings.

5. Local Storage

User preferences (theme, language, audio settings, recent file metadata) are saved exclusively on your local device storage and can be wiped anytime via app settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our official app distribution channel or developer contact.
